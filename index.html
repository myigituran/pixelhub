Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play?
Tap anywhere on the screen to jump over obstacles. The longer you survive, the faster the game gets.
How do I remove ads?
Open the Shop from the home screen and purchase "Remove Ads" — a one-time purchase that removes ads permanently.
I made a purchase but it's not showing up. What do I do?
Try the "Restore Purchases" button in the Shop screen. If the issue continues, email us with your purchase receipt and we'll help right away.
How do I delete my data?
Jump Dash stores your high score and settings only on your own device. Deleting the app removes all local data. For any other data questions, contact us directly.
