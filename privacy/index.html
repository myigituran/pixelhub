Privacy Policy

Jump Dash · Last updated August 1, 2026

Overview

Jump Dash ("the App") is developed by Pixelhub ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the App collects, how it is used, and who it is shared with. The App does not require you to create an account and does not collect your name, email address, or any other information that directly identifies you.

Information we collect

Gameplay data. Your high score and game settings are stored locally on your device only. We do not receive or store this data on our own servers.

Advertising identifiers. The App shows ads using Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier and general usage data to show and measure ads, and — where you have given permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt — to personalize ads based on your activity across other apps and websites. You can decline this permission at any time in your device's Settings, and the App will continue to work normally.

Purchase data. If you buy the "Remove Ads" option, the purchase is processed by Apple's App Store (or Google Play) and by RevenueCat, which we use to verify and manage purchase status across your devices. RevenueCat receives an anonymous purchase/subscriber identifier and transaction data needed to confirm your purchase — it does not receive your name, email, or payment details.

Third-party services we use

  • Google AdMob — advertising and ad measurement
  • RevenueCat — purchase management and validation
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — payment processing

Each of these providers has its own privacy policy governing how it handles data on our behalf.

Your choices

  • You can opt out of ad tracking via your device's Settings (App Tracking Transparency on iOS, Ads settings on Android).
  • Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored data (high score, settings) from your device.
  • You can restore a previous "Remove Ads" purchase at any time using the "Restore Purchases" button in the App.

Children's privacy

Jump Dash is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

Data retention & deletion

Gameplay data lives only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the App. To request deletion of any purchase or advertising-related data held by our third-party providers on your behalf, email us and we will assist you.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Email us and we'll get back to you.
